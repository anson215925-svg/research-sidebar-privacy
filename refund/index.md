# Research Sidebar Refund Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

See also: [Terms of Service](../terms/) · [Privacy Policy](../)

## 14-day refund on your first payment

If you're new to a paid plan (Pro or Ultra, monthly or yearly), you can get a **full refund within 14 days of your first payment**, for any reason. Your plan stops when the refund is made.

This also covers the right to cancel within 14 days that consumers have in the EU and UK.

## After 14 days

- **Cancelling:** you can cancel any time in **Settings → Plan → Manage subscription**. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again. We don't usually refund the rest of a period after the first 14 days.
- **Renewals:** if a yearly plan renewed and you didn't mean it to, ask within 14 days of the renewal charge and we will refund it, provided you've hardly used it since.
- **Upgrades:** when you move from Pro to Ultra, you're charged only the difference for the rest of the period. That charge is refundable within 14 days if you change your mind, and you then go back to Pro.
- **Problems on our side:** if the service doesn't work for you because of a fault we can't fix within a reasonable time, or we stop the service, we will refund the unused part of your period.
- **Invite codes** are free, so there's nothing to refund.

Nothing here limits refunds you are entitled to under the law where you live.

Email [anson215925@gmail.com](mailto:anson215925@gmail.com) with:
