# Research Sidebar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Research Sidebar ("the extension", "we", "us") is a Chrome extension that helps you read and understand research papers. It is based in Hong Kong. This policy explains what data the extension handles, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. It also serves as our Personal Information Collection Statement under Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance.

See also: [Terms of Service](terms/) · [Refund Policy](refund/)

## The short version

- The extension only reads a page or PDF when you ask it to. It does not collect your browsing history.
- Your reading history, saved papers, research focuses and settings stay in your browser.
- Text you choose to read, and your questions, are sent to an AI provider (Perplexity, or OpenAI if you sign in with ChatGPT) to write the answer.
- You don't need an account. Paid plans use a licence key, and Stripe handles payment.
- We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it for anything other than the extension's features.

## Data stored on your device

Saved in your browser's local extension storage and not sent to us:

- Reading history: paper titles, authors, links, DOIs, summaries, evidence labels and favourites.
- Research focuses, optional "AI memory" details you enter (for example your research field), citation style and other settings.
- Your agreement to the data notice, and when you gave it.
- A random install ID, and your licence key if you have one.
- If you connect ChatGPT, the sign-in tokens OpenAI issues to the extension.

You can remove single papers in the Reading History dashboard, or everything at once with **Settings → Privacy → Delete all my data**. Uninstalling the extension also deletes this data from your browser.

## Data sent for AI processing

Nothing is sent until you have agreed to the data notice in the extension. After that, when you read a page, paste a link or DOI, choose a PDF, ask the assistant a question, ask for research gaps or recommendations, or request a synthesis, the extension sends:

- The text of the paper or page you chose (excerpt, title, abstract, body text and links, within set length limits), and its address, link or DOI.
- Your question, the last few messages of the conversation, your active research focuses and any AI memory details you saved.
- Saved paper details (title, authors, year, aim, finding, limitations, relevance, tags) when you ask about your saved papers.

Which provider receives it depends on your setting:

- **Default (Perplexity):** requests go through our server, a Cloudflare Worker, to the [Perplexity API](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/legal/privacy-policy). Our server passes the request on and returns the answer. It does not store the content of your requests or keep a log of what you read. Cloudflare, which runs the server, may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the service ([Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)).
- **ChatGPT (optional):** if you connect your own ChatGPT account, requests go directly from your browser to OpenAI using your account, and our server is not involved ([OpenAI privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)).

Please don't send confidential material or anything that identifies patients or clients.

## Data used for limits and paid plans

- **Install ID:** a random ID sent with each request to our server so we can apply your daily action limit. Daily usage counts are deleted automatically within 2 days.
- **Licence key and subscription:** if you buy a plan or redeem an invite code, our server stores your licence key with your plan, its end date, the Stripe subscription ID, and the install IDs of the browsers using it (up to 3). We keep this while the plan is active and for up to 12 months after it ends, for support, refunds and our accounting records, unless the law requires longer.
- **Payments:** payments are processed by Stripe. When Stripe Managed Payments is on, Link (a Stripe company) is the seller of record. Stripe or Link receives your email address and payment details to take payment, send receipts, handle tax and prevent fraud. We never see your full card number. See [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) and [Link's privacy policy](https://link.com/privacy).
- **When you agree to the Terms at checkout**, we record that you agreed and which version, on the Stripe payment record.

## Who receives data

Only the service providers needed to run the features you use: Cloudflare (our server), Perplexity or OpenAI (AI answers), and Stripe or Link (payments). They may process data outside Hong Kong, including in the United States. We do not otherwise share or transfer your data, except where the law requires it.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your data or transfer it to third parties, except the providers listed above to perform what you asked for.
- We do not use or transfer your data for advertising or personalised ads, or to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
- We do not let humans read your data, except where you send it to us for support, where it is needed for security, or where the law requires it.

The use of information received from Chrome extension features follows the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Why we handle this data

To provide the features you ask for, apply fair-use limits, take payment and keep your plan working, give support, and meet legal and accounting duties. Supplying page text and questions is needed for the AI features to work. If you don't agree, the AI features stay off, and you can still use the sidebar to collect papers.

## Your rights

You can ask to access or correct the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. People in the EU, UK and some other places also have rights to object, restrict processing, get a copy of their data, and complain to their local data protection authority. In Hong Kong, you can contact the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data](https://www.pcpd.org.hk/).

Most data lives only in your browser, so you control it directly. For data on our server or payment records, email us at the contact below with your licence key. We reply within 30 days.

## Security

All data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Server data is kept to a minimum and protected by access controls. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

## Permissions

- **Side panel:** shows the Research Sidebar next to the page.
- **Active tab, scripting and site access:** let the extension read the paper page or link you choose. Papers are hosted on many different websites, so access is not limited to specific sites, but nothing is read until you act.
- **Storage:** saves your history and settings on your device.
- **Identity:** used only if you choose to connect ChatGPT through OpenAI's sign-in page.

## Children

The extension is not for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and the date above, and ask you again in the extension before any new kind of data is used.
